# Exam facts (with sources)

Only facts from official UMTYMP sources go here, each with the date it was checked. Anything else is an assumption
and must say so.

| Fact | Value | Source | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exam name | UMTYMP Algebra Qualifying Exam (also called Algebra Entrance Exam) | [What's on the exam](https://cse.umn.edu/mathcep/whats-umtymp-algebra-qualifying-exam) | 2026-11-03 |
| Format | Quantitative comparison: two quantities (a) and (b); choose (a) greater, (b) greater, equal, or not enough information | same | 2026-11-03 |
| Length | 50 questions | same | 2026-11-03 |
| Pace | "An average of 30 seconds" per question | same | 2026-11-03 |
| What it stresses | Accuracy, understanding, speed; built on pre-algebra knowledge; measures speed and reasoning more than any topic | same | 2026-11-03 |
| Study material | UMTYMP recommends no specific material; registrants get a practice-problem link ~2 weeks before their exam | same | 2026-11-03 |
| Public sample questions | 2 (on the page above) | same | 2026-11-03 |
| Who can take it | Students in grades 5–7 | [How to join](https://cse.umn.edu/mathcep/how-join-umtymp) | 2026-11-03 |
| Passing score | No set passing line; the exam score is the most important admission factor | [How to join](https://cse.umn.edu/mathcep/how-join-umtymp) | 2026-11-03 |
| Older students | Grades 7–9 may instead test into Calculus I (a different exam, out of scope) | [How to join](https://cse.umn.edu/mathcep/how-join-umtymp) | 2026-11-03 |

## Assumptions (not confirmed)

- **Retakes:** reportedly at most 2 attempts per year (search snippet only; see the
  [retesting page](https://cse.umn.edu/node/116691)).
- **Total time 25 minutes.** Derived from 50 × 30 s; the official page gives only the average.
- **Wrong-answer penalty.** Unknown; affects guessing advice. Don't give guessing advice until confirmed.
- **Order of difficulty within the exam.** Unknown.
